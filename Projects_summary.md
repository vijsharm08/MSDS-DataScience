This project focused on leveraging healthcare claims, eligibility, and utilization data to identify members at risk of becoming high-cost patients. The solution demonstrated how advanced analytics can assist healthcare organizations in population health management and value-based care initiatives.

Key Skills: Predictive Analytics, Healthcare Analytics, Machine Learning, Feature Engineering, Model Evaluation

Technologies: Python, Pandas, Scikit-Learn, XGBoost, Azure Databricks
